Boat tips / Coatings and Paint

When should I repaint my topsides instead of just polishing?

Most likely causes

  • Gelcoat worn through, with the fiberglass weave showing
  • Deep chalking that doesn't respond to compounding
  • Cracks, crazing, or fading across large areas
  • Stains that won't come out

The fix

Gelcoat usually lasts 10 to 20 years with care, and a good two-part polyurethane topside paint lasts 5 to 10. If polishing restores the shine, there's no need to repaint.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the hull shows blisters, soft spots, or widespread flaking. Hull damage gets more expensive the longer it waits.
