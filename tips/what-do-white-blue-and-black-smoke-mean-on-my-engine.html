What do white, blue, and black smoke mean on my engine?

Most likely causes

  • White: water or coolant burning, or condensation on a cold start. Persistent white can mean a blown head gasket or cracked block
  • Blue: oil burning. Normal on 2-strokes, but on a 4-stroke it means worn rings or valve seals
  • Black: running too rich from a bad choke, clogged air filter, or carb or injector problem

The fix

Blue smoke on a 4-stroke or persistent white smoke needs a mechanic. For black smoke, check the air filter, choke, and fuel delivery.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the engine still won't run after the fixes above, you smell fuel in the bilge, or there's smoke or overheating.
