Boat tips / Engine and Fuel

Why won't my outboard start? It cranks but doesn't fire.

Most likely causes

  • Bad or stale fuel (older than 30 days, especially ethanol blends). The #1 cause
  • Clogged fuel filter or water separator
  • Primer bulb not pumped firm
  • Fouled spark plugs
  • Portable tank vent left closed, creating a vacuum

The fix

Squeeze the primer bulb, open the tank vent, check fuel freshness, replace the fuel filter, and pull the plugs. Replace them if black, oily, or worn.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the engine still won't run after the fixes above, you smell fuel in the bilge, or there's smoke or overheating.
