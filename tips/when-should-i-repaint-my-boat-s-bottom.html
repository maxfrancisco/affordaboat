Boat tips / Coatings and Paint

When should I repaint my boat's bottom?

Most likely causes

  • Ablative (self-polishing) paint: every 1 to 3 years depending on product and use
  • Hard (modified epoxy) paint: every 1 to 2 years, can be scrubbed and reused
  • Trailered boats in freshwater rarely need bottom paint

The fix

Repaint when growth returns quickly after cleaning, paint is thin or chalky, the underlying color shows through, or barnacles stick easily. Warm saltwater like Florida wears paint faster and grows fouling faster.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the hull shows blisters, soft spots, or widespread flaking. Hull damage gets more expensive the longer it waits.
