How often should I service my outboard engine?

Most likely causes

  • Every 100 hours or once a year, whichever comes first, for most 4-strokes
  • Annually for most recreational use

The fix

Service includes engine oil and filter, lower unit gear oil, spark plugs, fuel filter, water pump impeller check, anodes, grease points, and a check of belt or timing components. Follow your owner's manual and keep records for warranty and resale.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if you find metal in the gear oil, water in the lower unit, or you're not comfortable doing the work yourself.
