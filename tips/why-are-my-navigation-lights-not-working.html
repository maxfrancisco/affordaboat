Why are my navigation lights not working?

Most likely causes

  • Burned-out bulb or failed LED
  • Corroded sockets and connections (very common in saltwater)
  • Blown fuse or tripped breaker
  • Bad ground wire
  • Faulty switch

The fix

Check the fuse, then the bulb, then test the socket for 12V. Clean contacts with a wire brush and apply dielectric grease. Lights are legally required between sunset and sunrise, so fix this before night operation.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if you see melted wire, smell burning, or the problem returns after cleaning connections. Never guess with electrical faults on a boat.
