Why is my outboard losing power or not reaching full RPM?

Most likely causes

  • Wrong or damaged propeller (bent blade, wrong pitch, spun hub)
  • Fouled hull or lower unit
  • Clogged filter or weak fuel pump
  • Overheating that triggers power-limiting mode
  • Overloaded boat or poor trim

The fix

Inspect the prop, check wide-open-throttle RPM against the manufacturer's range, clean the hull, and replace fuel filters. On EFI engines, check for stored fault codes.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the engine still won't run after the fixes above, you smell fuel in the bilge, or there's smoke or overheating.
