Why is there milky or leaking gear oil in my lower unit?

Most likely causes

  • Failed prop shaft seals, often from fishing line wrapped around the shaft
  • Damaged drain or vent plug gaskets
  • Cracked housing from impact
  • Corrosion around the seals or shaft

The fix

Milky oil means water intrusion, so don't ignore it. Drain, inspect for metal particles, replace the seals, and pressure test the unit. Catching it early can save the gears and bearings.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if you find metal in the gear oil, water in the lower unit, or you're not comfortable doing the work yourself.
