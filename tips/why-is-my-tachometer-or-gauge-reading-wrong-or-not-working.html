Why is my tachometer or gauge reading wrong or not working?

Most likely causes

  • Loose or corroded wires behind the dash
  • Bad ground
  • Failed sender (temp, fuel, or oil pressure)
  • Wrong tach signal setting (pulse or cylinder count)
  • Failing gauge or rectifier

The fix

Inspect wiring and ground, clean connections, and verify the tach setting for your engine. Test the sender with a multimeter or swap it. Never ignore a real oil or temperature warning without confirming it's a gauge fault.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if you see melted wire, smell burning, or the problem returns after cleaning connections. Never guess with electrical faults on a boat.
