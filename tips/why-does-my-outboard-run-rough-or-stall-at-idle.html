Why does my outboard run rough or stall at idle?

Most likely causes

  • Dirty carburetor or injectors from ethanol varnish
  • Fouled or wrong-gap spark plugs
  • Idle speed set too low or a faulty idle circuit
  • Air leak at a cracked intake hose or loose carb mount
  • Water in the fuel

The fix

Replace the plugs, add a marine fuel system cleaner, and replace the fuel filter. If it persists, the carb or injectors need professional cleaning.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the engine still won't run after the fixes above, you smell fuel in the bilge, or there's smoke or overheating.
