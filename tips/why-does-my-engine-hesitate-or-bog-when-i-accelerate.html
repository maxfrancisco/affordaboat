Why does my engine hesitate or bog when I accelerate?

Most likely causes

  • Lean mixture from a clogged jet, filter, or weak pump
  • Fouled plugs or a weak coil
  • Old fuel
  • Throttle cable out of adjustment or sticking linkage
  • Failing fuel or lift pump (common on inboards)

The fix

Replace the filter and plugs, use fresh fuel, check the throttle linkage, and confirm fuel pressure on EFI engines.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the engine still won't run after the fixes above, you smell fuel in the bilge, or there's smoke or overheating.
