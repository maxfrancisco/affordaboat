Boat tips / Maintenance

Why isn't water coming out of my outboard's tell-tale (pee stream)?

Most likely causes

  • Clogged tell-tale outlet
  • Worn or failed water pump impeller
  • Blocked water intake on the lower unit
  • Stuck thermostat
  • Cracked or leaking water tube

The fix

Shut the engine off. Clear the outlet with a thin wire or compressed air, check the intake screens, and replace the impeller if it's more than a couple of years old. Running without water flow can ruin an engine in minutes.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if you find metal in the gear oil, water in the lower unit, or you're not comfortable doing the work yourself.
