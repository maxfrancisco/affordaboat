Boat tips / Engine and Fuel

Why won't my inboard engine start? It cranks but doesn't fire.

Most likely causes

  • Closed fuel valve, clogged filter, or failed fuel pump
  • Bad or stale gas
  • No spark: worn plugs, bad coil, cracked distributor cap, failed ignition module
  • Flooded engine from over-cranking or a stuck choke or carb float
  • Engine kill or safety switch fault

The fix

Check the fuel valve and filter, then confirm fresh fuel. Pull a plug, ground it, and check for spark. If flooded, hold the throttle wide open and crank 10 seconds. Always run the blower 4 minutes before any restart attempt.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the engine still won't run after the fixes above, you smell fuel in the bilge, or there's smoke or overheating.
