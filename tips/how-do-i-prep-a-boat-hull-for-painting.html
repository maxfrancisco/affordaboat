How do I prep a boat hull for painting?

Most likely causes

  • Wash and degrease with a marine solvent
  • Sand to remove gloss (grit depends on the paint; check the label)
  • Repair cracks, gouges, and blisters with epoxy filler
  • Clean again and wipe with solvent right before painting
  • Prime with the system-recommended primer
  • Paint within the right temperature and humidity window

The fix

Prep is 80% of a lasting paint job. Using one manufacturer's system from primer to topcoat avoids compatibility problems.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the hull shows blisters, soft spots, or widespread flaking. Hull damage gets more expensive the longer it waits.
