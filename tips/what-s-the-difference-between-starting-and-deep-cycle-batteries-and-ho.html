What's the difference between starting and deep-cycle batteries, and how should I wire two?

Most likely causes

  • Starting (cranking) batteries deliver a big burst of power for a short time
  • Deep-cycle batteries deliver steady power for hours and tolerate discharge
  • Dual-purpose batteries do both, but neither perfectly

The fix

For most boats, use a starting battery for the engine and a separate deep-cycle for electronics, linked with a 1-2-Both-Off switch or an automatic charging relay so both charge from the engine but stay isolated when discharging.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if you see melted wire, smell burning, or the problem returns after cleaning connections. Never guess with electrical faults on a boat.
