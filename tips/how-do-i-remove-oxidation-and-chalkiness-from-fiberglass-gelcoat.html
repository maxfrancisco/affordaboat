How do I remove oxidation and chalkiness from fiberglass gelcoat?

Most likely causes

  • UV exposure breaking down the gelcoat surface
  • No regular waxing
  • Harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners
  • Storage in direct sun without a cover

The fix

Wash with marine boat soap, use an oxidation remover, then compound and polish. Finish with marine wax or a ceramic coating. Wax twice a year (more in Florida). Heavy oxidation may need wet sanding first.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the hull shows blisters, soft spots, or widespread flaking. Hull damage gets more expensive the longer it waits.
