What causes osmotic blisters on a fiberglass hull?

Most likely causes

  • Water absorbed through the gelcoat into the laminate, reacting with resin to form fluid-filled pockets
  • Boats kept in the water year-round with no barrier coat
  • Poor original lamination or moisture-prone resin

The fix

Small blisters can be ground, dried, and filled. Widespread blistering means peeling the gelcoat, drying the hull completely, and applying an epoxy barrier coat system. Get a surveyor or yard to confirm severity first.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the hull shows blisters, soft spots, or widespread flaking. Hull damage gets more expensive the longer it waits.
