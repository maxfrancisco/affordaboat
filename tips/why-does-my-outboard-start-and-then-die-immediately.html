Why does my outboard start and then die immediately?

Most likely causes

  • Fuel starvation from a clogged filter, kinked line, or failing pump
  • Dirty carburetor that can't hold idle
  • Choke or primer issues (too lean or too rich)
  • Faulty kill switch or ignition part
  • Overheat or low-oil protection shutting it down

The fix

Inspect fuel lines for kinks or cracks, replace the filter, and check that the primer bulb stays firm while running. Confirm cooling water flow and oil level, and look for warning alarms.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the engine still won't run after the fixes above, you smell fuel in the bilge, or there's smoke or overheating.
