How do I winterize an outboard or inboard boat?

Most likely causes

  • Stabilize the fuel and run the engine to circulate it
  • Change oil, filter, and lower unit gear oil
  • Fog the cylinders (outboards)
  • Flush the cooling system; on inboards, drain the block and manifolds or fill with non-toxic antifreeze
  • Remove and charge the battery
  • Clean and cover the boat so it can drain and breathe

The fix

South Florida rarely sees hard freezes, but storage prep still matters for long periods of non-use.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if you find metal in the gear oil, water in the lower unit, or you're not comfortable doing the work yourself.

How often should I service my outboard engine?

Why is my boat steering stiff or hard to turn?

Why is there milky or leaking gear oil in my lower unit?
