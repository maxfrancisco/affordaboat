Boat tips / Coatings and Paint

Can I put regular bottom paint on an aluminum boat?

Most likely causes

  • Most bottom paints contain copper, which causes severe galvanic corrosion on aluminum hulls, outboard lower units, and drives

The fix

Use a copper-free antifouling paint made for aluminum, or a barrier-coat system. Read labels carefully, including for outboard and sterndrive lower units, where only specific coatings are safe.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the hull shows blisters, soft spots, or widespread flaking. Hull damage gets more expensive the longer it waits.
