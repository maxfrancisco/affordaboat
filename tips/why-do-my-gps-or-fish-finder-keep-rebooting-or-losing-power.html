Why do my GPS or fish finder keep rebooting or losing power?

Most likely causes

  • Voltage drop from undersized wire or a long run
  • Loose or corroded connectors
  • Weak battery that sags under load, especially at engine start
  • Shared circuit with high-draw gear (trim, windlass, stereo)
  • Bad ground

The fix

Measure voltage at the unit during operation. Use a dedicated fused circuit, upsize the wire gauge, or add a separate house battery to isolate electronics from the starter.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if you see melted wire, smell burning, or the problem returns after cleaning connections. Never guess with electrical faults on a boat.
