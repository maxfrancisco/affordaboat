Why won't my outboard start when it's hot?

Most likely causes

  • Vapor lock: fuel boils in the lines, common in South Florida heat
  • Flooded engine from over-priming or too much choke
  • Ignition coil or stator that fails when hot
  • Weak fuel pump under heat

The fix

Let the engine cool 10 to 15 minutes. If flooded, open the throttle fully (no choke) and crank to clear the fuel. Squeeze the primer bulb to push out vapor. If it repeats, have the ignition tested.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the engine still won't run after the fixes above, you smell fuel in the bilge, or there's smoke or overheating.
