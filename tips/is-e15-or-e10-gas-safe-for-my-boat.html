Is E15 or E10 gas safe for my boat?

Most likely causes

  • E10 (10% ethanol) is approved by most manufacturers, but it attracts moisture and degrades quickly
  • E15 (15% ethanol) is not approved for marine engines and can void warranties and damage fuel systems
  • Ethanol-free recreational-grade fuel (90 to 93 octane) is best for storage and older boats

The fix

Use E10 or less, buy from high-turnover stations, add a stabilizer with ethanol treatment, and keep tanks nearly full in storage. Older fiberglass tanks and rubber lines are especially vulnerable.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the engine still won't run after the fixes above, you smell fuel in the bilge, or there's smoke or overheating.
