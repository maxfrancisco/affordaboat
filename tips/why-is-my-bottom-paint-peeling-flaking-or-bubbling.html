Boat tips / Coatings and Paint

Why is my bottom paint peeling, flaking, or bubbling?

Most likely causes

  • Poor surface prep (oil, wax, or gloss not removed)
  • Incompatible paint layers
  • Moisture in the hull or osmotic blistering
  • Too many layers built up over the years
  • Painting in the wrong temperature or humidity

The fix

Strip back to a sound surface, sand, clean with the recommended solvent, apply a compatible primer or barrier coat, and follow the temperature and recoat window on the label.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the hull shows blisters, soft spots, or widespread flaking. Hull damage gets more expensive the longer it waits.
