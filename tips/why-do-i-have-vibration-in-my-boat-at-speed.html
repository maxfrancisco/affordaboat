Boat tips / Maintenance

Why do I have vibration in my boat at speed?

Most likely causes

  • Bent or damaged propeller
  • Fishing line or debris wrapped around the shaft
  • Bent prop shaft or worn cutlass bearing (inboards)
  • Misaligned engine or drive coupling
  • Loose or worn motor mounts
  • Fouled hull with uneven growth

The fix

Inspect and balance or replace the prop, clear the shaft of any line, and check alignment and mounts. Vibration left unchecked destroys seals, bearings, and mounts.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if you find metal in the gear oil, water in the lower unit, or you're not comfortable doing the work yourself.
