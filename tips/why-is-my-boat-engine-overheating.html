Why is my boat engine overheating?

Most likely causes

  • Worn water pump impeller (the most common cause)
  • Clogged water intake or cooling passages
  • Stuck or failed thermostat
  • Closed seacock or clogged raw-water strainer (inboards)
  • Low coolant on closed-cooling inboards

The fix

Shut the engine off immediately. Check the intake and strainer, and confirm a strong tell-tale stream. Replace the impeller every 1 to 2 years and flush the engine after every saltwater use.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the engine still won't run after the fixes above, you smell fuel in the bilge, or there's smoke or overheating.
