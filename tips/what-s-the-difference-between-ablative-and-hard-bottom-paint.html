Boat tips / Coatings and Paint

What's the difference between ablative and hard bottom paint?

Most likely causes

  • Ablative slowly wears away to release biocide. Best for boats used regularly or kept in a slip, and recoats without much sanding
  • Hard paint doesn't wear and stays on the hull. Best for fast boats or boats frequently hauled and scrubbed, but needs sanding before recoat

The fix

Use ablative for slow-to-moderate cruisers and slip boats, hard paint for faster boats. Never mix types without checking compatibility, and use a barrier coat if there's any osmosis risk.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if the hull shows blisters, soft spots, or widespread flaking. Hull damage gets more expensive the longer it waits.
