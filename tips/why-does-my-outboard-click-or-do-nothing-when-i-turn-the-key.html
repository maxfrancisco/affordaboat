Why does my outboard click or do nothing when I turn the key?

Most likely causes

  • Dead or weak battery (should read 12.4V or higher)
  • Corroded or loose battery terminals
  • Engine not in neutral (neutral safety switch)
  • Kill switch lanyard not attached
  • Bad starter solenoid or starter motor
  • Blown ignition fuse

The fix

Clean terminals, charge or test the battery, confirm neutral, reattach the kill switch clip, then check fuses. A single click usually points to the solenoid or starter.

When to call a mechanic

Call a professional if you see melted wire, smell burning, or the problem returns after cleaning connections. Never guess with electrical faults on a boat.
